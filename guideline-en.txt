## Rights
Rights to the sound (audio data, lyrics, etc.) are owned by BCNO (songs provided to other artists are an exception)
Rights to the visuals (illustrations, compositing) are owned by everyone involved in producing them
Some songs are entrusted to JASRAC and NexTone. When using them, please check the relevant rights and follow the required procedures

## Credits when you publish
On any platform, please include these three items: credits for everyone involved in the original work / the X (Twitter) URLs of all of them / a direct URL to the original work
You can prepare them easily by copying and pasting the description of the original work
On Niconico Douga, please register the original work as the parent work in the content tree
Please refrain from re-posting translated videos from other channels or accounts

## Contacting us
Non-commercial use (an individual or an unincorporated group publishing for a hobby, basically free of charge) needs no contact; feel free to use the works
Commercial use (use by companies, and anything other than non-commercial use) requires prior contact by email to officialbcno@gmail.com

## When publishing on YouTube
Please turn monetization off (including Super Chat and similar)
If you use an off-vocal track other than the one published by BCNO, please credit the creator of that off-vocal track in the description or similar
Uses that earn more than the cost of the activity (CD releases and distribution, streaming distribution, paid live performances, etc.) are treated as commercial use

## Secondary works that are not accepted
Works with content against public order and morals, or that significantly destroy the world of the original
Works that are intended or likely to infringe the rights of others (individuals or groups)
Works that are intended or likely to make others (individuals or groups) uncomfortable

## About this guideline
Publishing a secondary work means you are considered to agree with all of the provisions
The guideline may change without notice
